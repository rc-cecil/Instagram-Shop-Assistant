# Design direction

Quiet shop operations: warm ivory canvas, deep green navigation, restrained sage
surfaces and real product photography. Typography uses Manrope for headings and
DM Sans for readable controls and records. Status names communicate the actual
workflow stage, especially pending evidence and owner verification.

Responsive grids become compact mobile columns; the navigation becomes a drawer.
Inputs have visible labels, controls have keyboard focus, and reduced-motion
preferences disable transitions. Monetary values use Ghana cedis and timestamps
use Africa/Accra. Destructive or uncertain actions remain explicit and reviewable.
