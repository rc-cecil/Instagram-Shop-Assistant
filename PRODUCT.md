# Mivelle shop operations

An invite-only owner dashboard for the Instagram shop `_testing.account1`.
The system database preserves product facts, photo usage,
conversations, orders and explicit payment decisions. The owner controls launch,
customer takeover, catalog approval and every payment verification.

The product serves a Ghana-based operator on desktop and mobile. The primary
tasks are reviewing pending payments, managing verified products, responding to
blocked conversations and supervising publishing. Never present an uncertain
external action as complete. Customer DMs use webhooks at all hours; publishing
uses five Ghana-time slots. SHEIN fulfilment is a manual task.
